Valence band, electron correlation, and possible temperature dependent change in the electronic structure near $E_F$ of a candidate of ambient-pressure nickelate superconductors Sr$_3$Ni$_2$O$_5$Cl$_2$

Ryota Goban, Daiki Ootsuki, Kenichi Ozawa, Akira Yasui, Masayuki Ochi, Kazuhiko Kuroki, Ryo Matsumoto, Kensei Terashima, Yoshihiko Takano, Yuji Muraoka, Takayoshi Yokoya

arXiv:2611.30088·cond-mat.supr-con·Published 2026-11-23

Photoemission spectroscopy (PES) studies of Sr$_3$Ni$_2$O$_5$Cl$_2$ (Sr3252), a candidate ambient-pressure bilayer nickelate superconductor, have been performed to investigate the electronic structure. Valence band spectra, which are compared with DFT and DFT+U calculations, reveal dominant Ni 3$d$ $t_{2g}$-derived states located around 0.6 eV binding energy with a shoulder around 0.2 eV that can be attributed to states with a dominant Ni 3$d_{3z2-r2}$ character. The overall valence band shape is better reproduced by the simulated spectrum of DFT+U (= 3 eV), providing an experimental estimation of the strength of electron correlation. High-resolution PES near the Fermi level ($E_F$) exhibits marked temperature dependence, which cannot be fully understood by a broadening due to coupling of a photo-hole to phonons alone and suggests change in electronic structure. The results provide fundamental electronic structure information of Sr3252, which is useful to further explore the superconductivity of Sr3252.
