U-HamGNN: a universal model for magnetic Hamiltonians conditioned on spin and Hubbard U

Hongyu Yu, Yinze Hu, Yizhou Chen, Yang Cheng, Yingwei Chen, Xingao Gong, Yang Zhong, Hongjun Xiang

arXiv:2611.20035·physics.comp-ph·Published 2026-11-15

The electronic structure of a magnetic crystal depends jointly on its spin configuration and the on-site Hubbard correction U, requiring a separate DFT+U calculation for each combination. We introduce U-HamGNN, an equivariant network that takes these two inputs directly and predicts spin-resolved Hamiltonians across diverse crystal chemistries. A spectral-hybrid optimizer that weights each update direction by how consistently the gradient points along it reaches 8% lower Hamiltonian error than Muon and 26% lower than AdamW in a ten-epoch benchmark. The predicted bands reproduce U-dependent gap opening and altermagnetic spin splitting within the trained window and beyond it; exchange couplings and magnetic-order calls derived from the Hamiltonian agree with DFT+U on a calibration set. This universal model provides a new tool for magnetic research.
