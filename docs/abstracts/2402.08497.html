Involutions in finite simple groups as products of conjugates

Daniele Dona, Martin W. Liebeck, Kamilla Rekvényi

arXiv:2402.08497·math.GR·Published 2024-02-13

Let $G$ be a finite non-abelian simple group, $C$ a non-identity conjugacy class of $G$, and $Γ_C$ the Cayley graph of $G$ based on $C \cup C^{-1}$. Our main result shows that in any such graph, there is an involution at bounded distance from the identity.
