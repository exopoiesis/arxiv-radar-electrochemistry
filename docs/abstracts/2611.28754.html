Synthesis Method-dependent Resist-like Behavior of Liquid Crystalline Graphene Oxide Films Under Electron Beam Irradiation

A. Sahakyan, Ghada E. Khedr, A. A. Hovhannisyan, G. Gevorgyan, K. Margaryan, G. Shakhatuni, M. Aleksanyan, Mohamed Aly Saad Aly, N. Nwaji, H. Gharagulyan

arXiv:2611.28754·cond-mat.mtrl-sci·Published 2026-11-21

Here, the electron-beam-induced structural evolution and patterning behavior of chitosan-functionalized GO films, specifically those using chemically vs electrochemically exfoliated GO, are investigated. Experimental results (supported by DFT calculations) revealed that electron irradiation drives controllable microstructure formation in GO by selectively triggering ablation and densification pathways, which are dictated by the GOs synthesis route and initial oxidation state. Furthermore, a comprehensive characterization study was conducted to compare the optostructural properties of the present system prior and subsequent to electron-beam irradiation. Moreover, a systematic study of the films mechanical properties across both micro- and macroscopic scales was also performed to elucidate the underlying mechanisms responsible for the observed phenomena. The results demonstrate that the integration of mask-free electron-beam patterning, multiscale characterization, and DFT modeling establishes a previously unexplored synthesis-structure-irradiation relationship in GO films, providing fundamental insights into irradiation-induced microstructure evolution and opening new avenues for the design of GO-based micro- and nanostructured materials.
