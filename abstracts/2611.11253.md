# Effect of Correlation on the Pressure-Induced Insulator to Metal Transition in Complex Spin-Orbit Coupled Double Perovskite La$_2$CoIr$_{1-x}$Ti$_x$O$_6$

**Authors:** Vineeta Yadav, Sromona Nandi, Rudra Sekhar Manna

We study the effect of electron correlation ($U$) and hydrostatic pressure on the electronic properties of double perovskites La$_2$CoIr$_{1-x}$Ti$_x$O$_6$ for $x$ = 0, 0.5, and 1, using first-principles electronic structure calculations within density functional theory (DFT). At ambient pressure, we systematically vary the electron correlation, i.e., on-site Coulomb interaction to study its effect on band gap and structural parameters. Increasing the value of $U$ enhances the band gap of all three compositions while producing only a slight variation in structural parameters. The effect of hydrostatic pressure is also studied for the same compositions. The estimated band gap values at ambient pressure closes at approximately 39, 42, and 53 GPa for $x$ = 0 (for $U_\mathrm{Co}$ = 5 eV and $U_\mathrm{Ir}$ = 2 eV), 0.5 (for $U_\mathrm{Co}$ = 5 eV and $U_\mathrm{Ir}$ = 2 eV), and 1 (for $U_\mathrm{Co}$ = 4 eV), respectively, indicating pressure-induced insulator-to-metal transitions. These results show that electron correlation stabilises the insulating state, whereas hydrostatic pressure favours the metallic state by closing the band gap. These findings highlight the tunability of correlated oxides through electron correlations and pressure, offering insights for materials design.
