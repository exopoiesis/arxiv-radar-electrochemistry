# Combined magnetic and structural transition state in first-principles calculations

**Authors:** Samare Rostami, Xavier Gonze

We present a generalized nudged elastic band (NEB) implementation within potential-based constrained density-functional theory (cDFT) for calculating transition pathways that simultaneously optimize atomic, lattice, and magnetic degrees of freedom. Unlike standard fixed-cell NEB calculations restricted to atomic displacements, our framework consistently couples atomic forces, stresses, and spin torques. The method supports fixed- and variable-cell approaches for collinear and non- collinear magnetic configurations, by incorporating magnetic torques and generalized cell coordinates within the NEB optimization. To validate the methodology, we present benchmark calculations for representative magnetostructural systems. Specifically, we investigate the bcc-to-hcp transformation in Fe and the antiferromagnetic-to-ferromagnetic transition in FeRh using the variable-cell formalism. Furthermore, we examine spin-reversal processes in two-dimensional CrI3 to analyze the influence of spin-orbit coupling on transition paths and their associated energy barriers.
